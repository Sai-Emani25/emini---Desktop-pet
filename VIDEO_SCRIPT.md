## EDITING NOTES

### Pacing
- Keep cuts snappy (every 3-5 seconds)
- Use quick transitions
- Maintain viewer attention

### Text Overlays
- Feature names when demonstrating
- Key benefits as bullet points
- URLs clearly visible
- Call-to-action at end

### Visual Effects
- Highlight cursor clicks
- Zoom into important text
- Use smooth camera movements
- Add subtle animations

### Accessibility
- Include captions/subtitles
- High contrast for readability
- Clear audio (no background noise)
- Screen reader friendly descriptions
